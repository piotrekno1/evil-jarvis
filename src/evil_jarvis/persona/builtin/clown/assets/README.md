# Chuckle recording

`chuckle.wav` is converted from the high-quality MP3 preview of
[Voices - male laughter - mild chuckles.wav by jodybruchon](https://freesound.org/people/jodybruchon/sounds/459455/),
published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Converted to 22,050 Hz, mono, 16-bit PCM with ffmpeg. The bundled recording is
unprocessed. During playback its quiet edges are trimmed, its level is matched
to the surrounding speech, and the clown's selected voice effect is applied
to both speech and chuckle together.

## Idle sounds

The idle chuckle reuses `chuckle.wav`. Two additional short, wordless recordings:

- `idle-sigh.wav`: [sigh.wav by Ashtalon](https://freesound.org/people/Ashtalon/sounds/275055/), CC0 1.0.
- `idle-hum.wav`: [Voice Male humming - Positive by bolkmar](https://freesound.org/people/bolkmar/sounds/469604/), CC0 1.0.

Both are converted from the high-quality MP3 previews to 22,050 Hz, mono,
16-bit PCM. Idle playback applies the selected voice effect, then lowers its
gain to 0.4 of the processed level. Original source recordings remain unprocessed.
