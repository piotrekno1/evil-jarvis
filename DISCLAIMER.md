# Safety and intended use

Evil Jarvis is an experimental Halloween entertainment project. Its characters
generate fictional dialogue. Speech recognition can misunderstand visitors, and
the language model can produce inaccurate, inappropriate, offensive or frightening
replies despite persona instructions and output checks. Those controls do not
guarantee suitable content, including for children.

Do not rely on a character for emergency help, supervision, professional advice,
or decisions affecting anyone's safety. It cannot reliably recognize distress
or replace a responsible adult.

## Operating an installation

- Have a responsible adult supervise interactions involving children. Make
  participation optional and stop the interaction if someone is distressed.
- Test the selected model, persona, sounds and speaker volume before use.
  Keep volume comfortable and provide distance from the speaker.
- Keep the keyboard or another practical way to stop playback accessible.
  Ctrl-C requests application shutdown; keep a speaker mute or power control
  available if immediate silence is needed.
- Secure props, wiring and equipment, and keep exits and walkways clear.
- Tell visitors that they are interacting with AI and that the microphone is
  used to process speech. Explain any recording or uploads before collecting
  visitor content. See the [privacy notes](README.md#privacy).

Operators are responsible for deciding whether their installation is appropriate
for its audience and for meeting applicable safety, privacy and venue requirements.
For a public or paid attraction, obtain advice appropriate to the location and use.
